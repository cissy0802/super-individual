Top-rated AI skills & best practices — one category at a time

> Roundup first, then the deep dives

Skills, plugins, rules and MCP servers that actually exist and are actually used, plus the best practices distilled by the vendors themselves. Each category opens with a roundup — what is out there, who ranks where, by what test, and when not to install any of it — followed by two to four deep dives into the entries worth the trouble.

House rules: nothing gets named unless it can be linked · every number carries its source and month · every issue states the honest cost · the takeaway is a test you can reuse, not a shopping list.
